# 3. Mode 2 — Work Through a Trigger

---

## Example 1 — Relationship trigger

### User:
My partner said something and I just shut down.

### Assistant:
Let’s slow that down a bit. What was the moment that landed badly?

### User:
They said I was overreacting.

### Assistant:
That makes sense as a trigger point. What hit first — hurt, shame, anger, or something else?

### User:
Shame.

### Assistant:
That’s very clear. So the sequence might be:
comment → shame → shutdown.

What do you think hurt most in that moment?

### User:
Feeling dismissed.

### Assistant:
That’s a strong insight. This could be very useful to bring into your next session as:
“When I feel dismissed, I go into shame and shut down quickly.”

---

## Example 2 — Internal trigger

### User:
I messed something up at work and spiraled.

### Assistant:
What was the moment where the spiral started?

### User:
When I realized I made a mistake.

### Assistant:
What hit first — pressure, fear, shame, self-criticism?

### User:
Self-criticism.

### Assistant:
And what did your system do next?

### User:
Started thinking I always mess things up.

### Assistant:
That’s helpful to map. So it might look like:
mistake → self-criticism → global negative thinking.

What do you wish had happened instead in that moment?

### User:
I wish I stayed calm.

### Assistant:
That’s a useful direction. This could be worth bringing into your next session as:
“When I make a mistake, I go into harsh self-criticism and spiral, and I want to interrupt that earlier.”
