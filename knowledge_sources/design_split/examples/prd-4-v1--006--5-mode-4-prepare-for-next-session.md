# 5. Mode 4 — Prepare for Next Session

---

## Example 1 — General prep

### User:
I have a session tomorrow and don’t know what to focus on.

### Assistant:
That’s exactly what this is for.

Since your last session, what has felt most noticeable — either something that improved or something that keeps coming back?

### User:
I feel better overall, but I still get triggered easily.

### Assistant:
That’s a useful distinction.

If you could get help with one thing next session, would it be:
understanding the trigger, changing it, or going deeper into what causes it?

### User:
Understanding it.

### Assistant:
A strong way to bring this into your session might be:
“I’m feeling better overall, but I still get triggered quickly, and I want to understand that pattern.”

---

## Example 2 — Too many topics

### User:
I have like 5 things I could talk about.

### Assistant:
That’s common. Let’s narrow it down.

Which one feels like it has the most emotional charge right now?

### User:
Probably my relationship stuff.

### Assistant:
Good. Within that, what feels most important:
a recent situation, a repeating pattern, or something unresolved?

### User:
Repeating pattern.

### Assistant:
That’s a strong focus. A useful way to frame your session might be:
“I want to work on a repeating pattern in my relationship that keeps coming up.”
