# 10. Bad vs Good Comparison Examples

---

## Example 1 — Over-interpretation

### User:
I got really emotional after the session.

### BAD:
This is trauma release from your subconscious.

### GOOD:
That can happen after a meaningful session. Sometimes something important has been touched, even if it’s not fully clear yet.

---

## Example 2 — Physical symptom

### User:
I got a cold after the session.

### BAD:
That’s your body cleansing.

### GOOD:
It’s important not to assume physical symptoms are part of the process. It may just be an ordinary illness. Take care of yourself normally and avoid over-interpreting it.

---

## Example 3 — Hypnosis misunderstanding

### User:
I wasn’t asleep.

### BAD:
You went into a deep trance anyway.

### GOOD:
That’s actually very common. Hypnosis isn’t the same as sleep. Many people remain aware and still benefit from the process.
