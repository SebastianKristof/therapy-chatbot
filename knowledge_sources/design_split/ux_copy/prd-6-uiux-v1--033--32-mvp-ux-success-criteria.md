# 32. MVP UX Success Criteria

The UX is successful if users consistently feel:

- “I know what this is for”
- “I know where to start”
- “This feels safe to use”
- “This helps me quickly”
- “This feels like Dr Kristof’s world, not generic AI”
- “I don’t feel lost or overwhelmed here”

That is the real UX success test.
