# 30. Writing / Microcopy Principles

All UI copy should be:

- clear
- calm
- direct
- psychologically literate
- not overly “marketing”
- not overly clinical
- not mystical
- not cute

Good product writing in this app should feel like:
> “confidently calm”

Examples of good microcopy tone:
- “What would help most right now?”
- “What feels most worth understanding?”
- “Something to bring into your next session”
- “A useful takeaway”

Bad tone examples:
- “Unlock your subconscious power”
- “Tell us your deepest truth”
- “Ready for transformation?”
- “Let’s dive in!”

That is the wrong feel.
