# 10. Primary Guided Actions

The home screen should prominently surface the 6 core modes:

- Reflect on what came up
- Work through a trigger
- Reinforce this week’s progress
- Prepare for next session
- Ask about hypnosis or the process
- Ask about what I’m experiencing

These should be visually clear and easy to tap.

This is the main product surface.
