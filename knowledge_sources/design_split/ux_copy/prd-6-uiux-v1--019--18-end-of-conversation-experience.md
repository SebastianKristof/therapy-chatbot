# 18. End-of-Conversation Experience

At the end of a meaningful interaction, the interface should help surface something like:

- **What stood out**
- **What may be worth bringing into your next session**
- **A useful takeaway**
- **A reflection summary**
- **A next-session note**

This is a major UX opportunity.

It turns “chat” into something more valuable and usable.
