# 2. Core UX Philosophy

AskDrKristof should not feel like:
- generic ChatGPT
- a support chatbot from a telecom company
- a productivity app
- a mystical wellness app
- a “talk to AI about anything” toy

It should feel like:
> **a private, structured therapeutic support companion**

The experience should communicate:
- containment
- clarity
- professionalism
- warmth without fluff
- seriousness without coldness
