# 5. Emotional Feel of the Product

If the interface had a personality, it should feel:

- calm
- quiet
- intelligent
- private
- grounded
- respectful
- steady
- not sterile
- not spiritualized
- not clinical in a hospital way

The emotional design should support:
> **psychological containment**

That is a very important design goal.
